Ab kya baaki hai 1 blocker · 4 optional

Code ka kaam khatam hai. Jo baaki hai woh aapke decision ka intezaar kar raha hai, ya jaan-boojhkar baad ke liye chhoda gaya hai.

Legal approval — paisa hold karne ki

Ye sabse zaroori hai. Module ab customer ka paisa escrow me rakhta hai, aur dispute me use freeze karke dobara baant-ta hai. India me customer ka paisa hold karne ke liye likhit legal confirmation chahiye hoti hai.

Code taiyaar hai aur test bhi ho chuka hai — bas ye confirmation aane tak flag on karna aapka decision hai. (BRD ka blocking question 8.)

Module flag abhi set nahi hai

Database me construction ka flag khaali hai. System khaali flag ko "on" maanta hai — matlab abhi ye by default chalu hai, bina kisi ne decide kiye.

Ise soch-samajhkar on ya off karein. Runbook me exact command likhi hui hai.

Contractor firm ke staff logins

Abhi ek contractor ka ek hi login hai. Badi firm ke supervisor ya accountant ke alag login abhi nahi bane.

Design already hai — project record me participant list bani hui hai, isliye baad me banane par koi migration nahi lagega. (BRD question 9.)

Materials aur labour management

Material kharidna, machinery rent, labour ka hisaab — ye BRD me mention nahi tha, isliye nahi banaya.

Agar chahiye to alag phase lagega. (BRD question 17.)

Project khatam hone par AMC offer

Aapka AMC module already hai. Handover ke waqt customer ko AMC ka offer dikhana ek achha idea hai, lekin abhi nahi bana.

Humara suggestion — offer dikhaayein, apne aap enrol na karein. Bina poochhe paid plan me daalna trust ka problem ban jaata hai. (BRD question 18.)

2 · Haath se — pura safar, 10 minute

Isi order me karein. Har step me jo hona chahiye woh green me likha hai.

  1. Customer bankar login karein Service strip me Construction ka tab dikhna chahiye.
  2. /construction kholein Photo ke saath catalogue, search box, aur neeche 4 tab — Home · Enquiries · My Quotes · Projects.
  3. "Browse verified contractors" pe click karein Verified contractor ki list. Kisi ek pe click karo — uska poora profile, purane kaam ki photo aur reviews khulne chahiye.
  4. Koi service chunkar enquiry bhejein Reference number milega, aur Enquiries tab me woh dikhne lagegi.
  5. Alag browser me /contractor/login pe contractor bankar login karein Alag login, alag screens. Enquiry unke feed me dikhegi — lekin customer ka naam nahi, sirf area.
  6. Lead accept karke quote banayein aur bhejein Exclusions likhe bina bhej hi nahi payenge. Bhejte hi customer ke My Quotes me aa jayega.
  7. Customer bankar quote accept karein Project ban jayega, status "Waiting for payment".
  8. Project me paisa daalein (Fund) Wallet ka balance same rahega, sirf locked amount badhega — paisa hold hua hai, gaya nahi.
  9. Doosre module (Food ya Taxi) me pura balance kharch karne ki koshish karein Refuse ho jana chahiye. Jo paisa hold me hai woh kahin aur nahi lag sakta.
  10. Contractor bankar stage submit karein — bina photo ke Refuse hoga. Photo daalkar dobara karein, tab jayega.
  11. Customer bankar stage approve karein Contractor ke wallet me turant paisa aa jayega (retention kaatkar). Dobara approve dabayein — doosri baar paisa nahi jayega.
  12. Project ke Documents tab me kuch upload karein, phir usi ka naya version daalein Purana version "Superseded" mark hoga, delete nahi hoga.
  13. Messages tab me message bhejein Contractor ki taraf dikhega. Stage approve wala system note bhi usi chat me dikhega.
  14. Kisi stage pe dispute utha kar dekhein Us stage ka paisa freeze ho jayega — na customer approve kar payega, na admin.
  15. Admin me /admin/construction/dashboard kholein Wahi dispute "Needs attention" me sabse upar dikhega, aur frozen amount ke saath.
  16. Admin se dispute resolve karein Jo bhi chunein — release, refund, ya aadha-aadha — hisaab poora milna chahiye, ek paisa idhar-udhar nahi.
  17. Admin me Payment Control aur Activity Record kholein Poora paisa jo hold me hai woh dikhega, aur upar ka har action activity record me mil jayega.
  18. Aakhir me: admin se construction module off karke dekhein Nayi enquiry ruk jayegi, lekin chalu project ka approve aur payment chalta rahega. Yahi sabse zaroori check hai.

Agar step 9, 11, 14 ya 18 me jo likha hai woh na ho — turant batayein. Ye chaar seedhe paise se jude hain, baaki sab uske baad aata hai.

Kuch purane bugs jo raaste me mile Doosre modules ke bhi

Ye construction banate waqt mile, lekin inme se kuch already live modules me the — matlab ye pehle se chal rahe the aur kisi ko pata nahi tha.

  • 15 unique index kabhi bane hi nahi the Bike-rent, service-provider, quick-commerce aur food — chaaron me. MongoDB `$ne` ko partial index me accept nahi karta, isliye poora index chupchaap reject ho jaata tha. Iska matlab: duplicate hub code, ek hi bike do baar register, ek payout do baar record, aur ek restaurant ka do baar billing — sab possible tha. Sab theek, 6 index build ho gaye.
  • Contractor aur service provider ko push notification jaati hi nahi thi Firebase ki owner list me dono ka naam hi nahi tha, aur error chupchaap nigal liya jaata tha. Ab dono register hain aur test bhi ho chuka.
  • Module off karne par project ka paisa phas jaata tha Sabse bada. Construction band karte hi na contractor kaam submit kar sakta tha, na customer approve — sirf cancel karke customer ko refund ho sakta tha. Matlab jis contractor ne kaam kar liya, use paisa milne ka koi raasta nahi bachta tha. Ab naya kaam rukta hai, purana project normally khatam hota hai.
  • JWT expiry setting galat thi Local `.env` me `7dj` likha tha — is galti se poore platform ka har login fail ho raha tha, sirf construction ka nahi.
  • Admin panel se do module chupchaap hat jaate the Koi bhi admin apni profile save karta to porter aur service-provider uski allowed list se nikal jaate the.
